What changed, and which finding it answers

FindingWhat this revision does
D1 header blockScore, label, weights and a two-sentence summary sit in one band above the evidence. The place name drops from 35px to 28px so the score is the largest number on the page.
D2 thin panelsPopulation and rent get a scaled three-row comparison against the state and the nation. No panel is now a number and a receipt.
D3 unlabelled seriesA legend under every two-series chart, and the delta text now describes the comparison the chart draws rather than the previous month.
D4 search everywhereThe search field is docked in the top bar, on every page, not only the home page.
D5 meaning without colourA mono better / worse word sits against the national figure. No hue is used to say good or bad anywhere.
D6 a faceSource Serif 4 for the place name, Inter Tight for the interface, both self-hosted. The serif is a choice rather than a default; the sans stops being the system stack.
D7 dead spaceThe top bar owns 56px and the crumb begins 16px under it. The empty band is gone.
D8 promoted receiptOne receipt at 13px in ink sits under the summary, before any panel. The per-panel receipts stay small, which is correct once the idea has been taught once.
